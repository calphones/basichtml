Three Timeless Truths

01

Anchored
in Faith

The anchor holds not in calm, but in the storm. Faith is not the absence of waves — it is the certainty that what carries you cannot sink.

02

Storms
Will Come

No promise was ever made of still waters. But every storm has a shore, and the One who sent you on this journey knows exactly where you are.

03

You Cannot
Be Sunk

The boat that carries God does not yield to storms. Not because the waves grow smaller, but because the hands that hold it are greater.
